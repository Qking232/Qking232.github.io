**Renewable Vietnam** is an information hub for Viet Nam's clean energy
transition — a clear, plain-language place to follow where the country's
electricity comes from, what is being built, and how the shift to solar, wind
and water is progressing toward 2030.

Viet Nam has one of the fastest-growing energy needs in the region. This site
collects progress, projects and outlook in one place, alongside an interactive
map of renewable potential across the country.
