# PM-24: Executive Steering Reporting, Lookahead & Escalation

- **Phase**: `Phase 4: Execution, Controls, Risk & Change (RIBA 5)`
- **RIBA Stage**: `RIBA Stage 5`
- **P6 Milestone Gate**: `Weekly / Monthly Cadence`
- **WBS Scope**: `All WBS`
