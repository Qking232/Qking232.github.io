# PM-22: Quality Assurance (QA/QC), ITPs & NCR / RFI Discipline

- **Phase**: `Phase 4: Execution, Controls, Risk & Change (RIBA 5)`
- **RIBA Stage**: `RIBA Stage 5`
- **P6 Milestone Gate**: `Site Construction`
- **WBS Scope**: `WBS 4.0-5.0`
- **Governing Standards**: ISO 9001 QA/QC, BCAR Inspection Plan, Trade ITPs
- **Focus Areas**: CQP Manual, Hold / Witness Points, Concrete / Weld NDE, NCR Log (Cat A/B/C), RFI Aging <= 5d

## Module Overview
Enforce Inspection & Test Plans, hold/witness points, concrete/weld QA logs, submittals SLAs, and timely Non-Conformance closure.

## Study Takeaway (`Why Each Document Matters`)
- **Doc 1 (Standard)**: Establish zero-defect construction discipline preventing hidden faults before Cx.
- **Doc 2 (Deliverable)**: Define concrete cube crush tests, MV/HV cable hipots, and third-party weld NDE rules.
- **Doc 3 (Template)**: Require CxA/RE physical sign-off before burying conduits, slabs, or pipework.
- **Doc 4 (Audit)**: Close overdue RFIs (<5 days) and open NCRs before they block handover certificates.
