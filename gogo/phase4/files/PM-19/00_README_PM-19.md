# PM-19: Earned Value Management (EVM): CPI, SPI, EAC & Recovery

- **Phase**: `Phase 4: Execution, Controls, Risk & Change (RIBA 5)`
- **RIBA Stage**: `RIBA Stage 5`
- **P6 Milestone Gate**: `Monthly Cutoffs`
- **WBS Scope**: `WBS 1.0-7.0`
- **Governing Standards**: ANSI/EIA-748 & PMI Practice Standard for EVM
- **Focus Areas**: CPI >= 0.92, SPI >= 0.90, EAC / VAC / TCPI, Rules of Credit, Acceleration Plan

## Module Overview
Measure physical % complete against PV/EV/AC, diagnose CPI/SPI drift early, and enforce schedule compression or fast-track recovery plans.

- **Doc 1 (Standard)**: Master PV, EV, AC, SPI, CPI, TCPI, and EAC formulas used to audit delivery health.
