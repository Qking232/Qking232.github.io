# PM-17: RFP Issuance, Technical Bid Evaluation (TBE) & BAFO Leveling

- **Phase**: `Phase 3: Procurement, Contracts & Supply Chain (RIBA 4)`
- **RIBA Stage**: `RIBA Stage 4`
- **P6 Milestone Gate**: `Pre-Award Gate`
- **WBS Scope**: `WBS 3.0-5.0`

Run blind commercial/technical scoring matrices, normalize exclusions/deviations, and lead structured BAFO negotiations.

## Study Takeaway (`Why Each Document Matters`)
- **Doc 1 (Standard)**: Guarantee transparent, defensible contractor selection under institutional governance.
- **Doc 2 (Deliverable)**: Issue standardized pricing schedules so bids can be compared apples-to-apples.
- **Doc 3 (Template)**: Plug gaps, price exclusions, and benchmark labour productivity rates across bidders.
- **Doc 4 (Audit)**: Document BAFO savings, negotiated risk caps, and final contract execution readiness.
