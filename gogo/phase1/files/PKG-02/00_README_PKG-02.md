# PKG-02: Site Selection, 45 MW Basis of Design & Battery Limits

- **Phase**: `Phase 1: Initiation, Charter & Business Case (RIBA 0-1)`
- **P6 Milestone Gate**: `A1020 / M1000 / M2110`
- **RIBA Stage**: `RIBA Stage 1-2`
- **Governing Standards**: I.S. EN 50600-1/-2-1/-2-2/-2-3, ISO/IEC 22237 Availability Class 3, Eurocode 7, OPW CFRAM Flood Maps, EirGrid ECP, ASHRAE TC 9.9, EU F-Gas 2024/573
- **WBS Scope**: `WBS 1.0-PERM & WBS 2.0-ENG`
- **Focus Areas**: Land parcel due diligence (Dublin/Kildare), 110kV grid connection, OPW flood risk (Zone C + 20% climate change), karst geotechnical mitigation, 45.0 MW critical IT sizing (5x 9 MW halls, 2x 63MVA, 20x 2.5MVA HVO, 45x 1000kVA UPS, 8x 1200RT chillers), PUE <= 1.25, and multi-party battery limits.

## Module Overview
PKG-02 bridges physical land due diligence with 45 MW critical facility engineering, translating tenant SLA requirements and Uptime Tier III availability into concrete Irish site specifications.
