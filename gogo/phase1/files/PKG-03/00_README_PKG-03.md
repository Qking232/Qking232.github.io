# PKG-03: Capex Economics, 10-Year DCF Model & Board FID Sanction

- **Phase**: `Phase 1: Initiation, Charter & Business Case (RIBA 0-1)`
- **P6 Milestone Gate**: `M1000 -> M7060`
- **RIBA Stage**: `RIBA Stages 0-7`
- **Governing Standards**: RICS NRM1, DCF Investment Appraisal, RIBA Plan of Work 2020 Data Centre Overlay, Stage-Gate Tranches TR-01..TR-06
- **WBS Scope**: `WBS 1.0 through 8.0 & All WBS`
- **Focus Areas**: RICS NRM1 elemental capex budget benchmarked in EUR/MW, 10-year DCF financial returns (8.5% WACC hurdle, IRR, NPV, Payback), multi-variable stress testing (Capex +/-10%, Power Tariff +/-15%, PUE 1.18-1.35), Pre-NTP Final Investment Decision (FID) Board Memorandum, Stage-Gate 1 Exit Criteria (20-point checklist), and Limited Notice to Proceed (LNTP) release authorization.

## Module Overview
PKG-03 consolidates capital cost planning, financial underwriting, and stage-gate governance into the final investment decision dossier presented to the Board of Directors for full project funding.
