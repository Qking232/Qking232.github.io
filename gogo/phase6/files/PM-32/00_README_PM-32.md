# PM-32: Operational Readiness & Transition (OR&T) & Black-Hat Drills

- **Phase**: `Phase 6: Handover, OR&T & Closeout (RIBA 6-7)`
- **RIBA Stage**: `RIBA Stage 6`
- **P6 Milestone Gate**: `A6510-A6540`
- **WBS Scope**: `WBS 6.5-OPS`

- **Focus Areas**: SOP / EOP / MOP Library, Black-Hat Outage Drills, 24/7 Crew Competency, OEM Critical SLAs, 2-Yr Spares Inventory

## Module Overview
Train 24/7 Critical Facilities staff, validate EOP/MOP/SOP libraries, run live Black-Hat incident drills, and stock 2-year critical spares.

## Study Takeaway (`Why Each Document Matters`)
- **Doc 1 (Standard)**: Avoid human-error outages (>70% of failures) through certified operating discipline.
- **Doc 2 (Deliverable)**: Equip shift engineers with step-by-step switching and emergency response guides.
- **Doc 3 (Template)**: Test live crew reactions to simulated tripped breakers, chiller leaks, and fire alarms.
- **Doc 4 (Audit)**: Ensure breakers, PLC cards, fan drives, and 4-hour vendor SLAs exist prior to Day-1.
